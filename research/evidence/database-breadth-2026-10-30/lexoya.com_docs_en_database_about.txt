Managed Databases
One-click managed database deployment with automated backups, multiple engine choices, and version selection.
What is a Managed Database?
Managed Databases are services that handle installation, configuration, backups, and maintenance automatically. You simply choose your engine and version — Lexoya takes care of the rest.
