## Scope and process

Search Iranian-location VPS (compute) tariffs in Persian and English, including fixed VPS, cloud and managed-VPS families. Managed VPS is not a managed database. Follow first-party family tabs, datacenter variants and public “more plans” stores; retain all qualifying fixed monthly-reference configurations returned, without a provider/configuration quota. Preserve other published billing periods in evidence without constructing unsupported monthly equivalents. Variable-resource unit rates alone do not manufacture fixed offers. A numeric starting price without identified resources is not an offer.

First broad queries were `خرید سرور مجازی ایران قیمت رم پردازنده SSD` and `سرور ابری ایران تعرفه قیمت منابع VPS`. Follow-up queries were `سرور مجازی ایران SSD قیمت ماهانه هاست نگار`, `سرور مجازی ایران قیمت ماهانه هاست دی ال پارس پک`, `سرور مجازی ایران 2 هسته 4 گیگابایت 30 SSD قیمت`, `سرور مجازی ایران NVMe قیمت ساعتی تعرفه ابر زس ابر دراک`, `سرور مجازی ایران خرید قیمت SSD پارس وی دی اس راست چین کلود`, and `سرور مجازی ایران قیمت هاست نگار سون هاست نت افراز`. Directed overlap queries then used `"سرور مجازی ایران" "تعرفه" "NVMe" "۱ هسته"`, `"سرور ابری ایران" "قیمت" "پیکربندی"`, and `"Iran VPS" "تومان" "SSD"`. Search snippets/directories supplied leads only; current retained first-party content controls admission. These rounds continued to add useful providers and resources, so the later search audit is required for the final stopping decision.

No accounts, login, cart additions, purchases or provider contact. Public order links are retained but were not followed when they would mutate a cart. HTTP 200 without actual tariff content is not verification. A failed request does not advance verification or imply withdrawal.

## Admission outcomes and exact identity

Unknown disk type, mixed media or source conflict does not have to hide a verified base tariff. The catalog admits those priced configurations with unresolved type, separate listing identity and prominent evidence status. This applies to Bertina, Shopingserver, Pouyasazan and Afagh, plus Famaserver's ambiguous/mixed-disk rows. Visnet's public product page is a valid general handoff despite empty per-plan buy links; disclose that destination rather than assert direct checkout. Mobinhost's two unambiguous unlimited base prices remain incomplete because selectable bandwidth is unresolved; ambiguous “million” price labels are held. These admission decisions refine conservative research holds under #12's explicit base-price/general-handoff contract; they do not resolve the underlying gaps.

All active new configurations retain public numeric tariffs and identified resource claims, with uncertain claims excluded from exact comparison,, retained first-party sources/check dates and a general source handoff. Mandatory extras remain unknown unless explicitly evidenced. No copied provider's traffic rule is generalized to another. Unsupported ingress/packet policies are preserved as facts and excluded from nonzero usage estimates, rather than converted to a false zero charge. Mixed-media aggregate GB is explicitly decimal derived arithmetic, with original components retained and no single-type grouping.
